GinX Privacy Notice

1. Scope of This Notice

This notice explains how GinX handles personal data relating to you when you visit ginx.io, use the Telegram Mini App, create or manage an account, complete identity verification, trade, participate in copy trading or promotions, use Partner Web, or contact us.

Some products, regions, or campaigns may have supplementary privacy notices. If a supplementary notice is inconsistent with this notice, the response from customer support at Telegram @ginxcn / @ginxen prevails.

2. Data We May Process

Depending on the services you use, we may process:

  • Contact data: email address, phone number, address, and communication preferences.
  • Biometric or liveness data: facial images and verification results used for identity verification, fraud prevention, or account recovery.
  • Account data: user ID, security settings, permissions, referral relationships, and account status.
  • Transaction and financial data: orders, deposits, withdrawals, wallet addresses, payment information, source of funds, and source of wealth.
  • Device and technical data: IP address, device identifier, operating system, browser, logs, cookies, login time, and approximate location.
  • Usage and behavioral data: page visits, feature use, clicks, preferences, and behavior signals relevant to account security or market integrity.
  • Communication data: support tickets, chats, emails, call recordings, complaints, and appeal materials.
  • Marketing data: subscription choices, campaign participation, and interactions with promotional content.
  • Referral and partner-relationship data: invitation codes, promotion links, attribution partners, agent IDs, hierarchical relationships, referral time, and referral status.
  • Partner operating data: commission contribution, rebates, marketing incentives, copy-trading revenue sharing, settlement records, tier, and assessment data.
  • Coupon and campaign data: coupon type, issuance source, activation and usage records, unlock progress, campaign eligibility, and anti-abuse review results.
  • Partner Web usage data: partner login, query, view, export, and other back-office operation logs.
  • Other data required by law or for a particular service.

3. Sources of Data

We may collect data directly from you, and data may be generated automatically when you use the services. For identity verification, sanctions screening, fraud prevention, payments, or blockchain analysis, we may also obtain data from actual supplier categories, affiliates, public sources, and partners.

If your account is attributed through an introducing broker, agent, channel partner, or another referral relationship, we may also obtain information required for referral relationships, promotions, customer support, or compliance review from the relevant partner.

When you use the Telegram Mini App, we may also obtain relevant data from the Telegram environment or technical interfaces provided by Telegram.

4. Why We Use Data

We may process personal data to:

  • Create, manage, and protect accounts.
  • Provide trading, transfers, copy trading, and other activated services.
  • Verify identity, conduct customer due diligence, and keep records current.
  • Prevent fraud, account takeover, money laundering, market manipulation, and other abuse.
  • Process orders, payments, customer support, complaints, and appeals.
  • Monitor and improve system performance, stability, and user experience.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, tax, audit, or law-enforcement requirements.
  • Send service notices and, where appropriately authorized, marketing messages.
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal rights.
  • Achieve other lawful purposes explained to you when data is collected.

5. Automated Tools and Risk Assessment

We may use automated tools to help identify identity fraud, account-security risk, abnormal trading, or other compliance risks. Automated results may trigger supplementary verification or manual review.

6. Data Sharing

To the extent necessary for the purposes above, we may provide data to:

  • Suppliers providing cloud services, cybersecurity, identity verification, payments, customer support, communications, analytics, or professional services.
  • Affiliates or partners involved in providing products to you.
  • Auditors, lawyers, insurance institutions, and other professional advisers.
  • Courts, regulators, law-enforcement, tax, or government authorities authorized by law.
  • Participants in restructuring, financing, acquisition, or asset-transfer transactions who owe appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • Other recipients indicated by you or authorized by you.

When your account is validly attributed through an introducing broker’s, agent’s, or channel partner’s exclusive link or invitation code, we may provide that partner with limited account and operating data necessary to manage the referral relationship, verify commissions, provide authorized support, and prevent campaign abuse.

7. Cross-Border Transfers

GinX and its suppliers may process data outside your region.

8. Data Security

We use organizational and technical measures appropriate to the nature and risk of the data to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse. No system can guarantee absolute security, so you should also enable available account-security features and protect your login credentials.

9. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as needed to achieve the processing purpose, fulfill legal and regulatory obligations, resolve disputes, and protect lawful interests. For retention periods, starting points, legal holds, and deletion exceptions for different data categories, see the Personal Data Retention and Privacy Requests Guide.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, data portability, withdrawal of consent, opt-out from particular marketing, and review of automated decisions.

These rights may be limited by anti-money-laundering records, legal holds, fraud investigations, other persons’ rights, and other statutory exceptions. For submission methods, see the Personal Data Retention and Privacy Requests Guide.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Depending on how you access GinX, we may use cookies, local storage, software development kits, device identifiers, logs, or similar technologies.

On the ginx.io website, these technologies may be used for login, security, preference storage, functional analytics, and, where appropriately authorized, marketing measurement.

In the Telegram Mini App, relevant technologies may include Telegram interfaces, Mini App local storage, session information, device and connection information, and may not operate as traditional browser cookies.

12. Minors

GinX services are not directed to minors. If you believe a minor has provided personal data to us, contact platform support through Telegram at @ginxcn / @ginxen.

13. Updates to This Notice

We may update this notice because of legal, technical, or business changes.

14. Contact and Complaints

For privacy questions or rights requests, contact us through Telegram at @ginxcn / @ginxen.
