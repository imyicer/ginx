Digital Assets and Trading Services Risk Disclosure

Important Notice

Digital assets and leveraged products involve high risk and are not suitable for all users. Prices may change sharply in a short period, and you may lose some or all of the funds you invest. Before using any GinX product, carefully assess your experience, financial condition, investment objectives, and risk tolerance, and obtain independent professional advice where necessary.

This disclosure cannot list every risk. The technical availability of a service does not mean that the service is legal, suitable, or aligned with your personal objectives in your region.

1. Price and Volatility Risk

Digital assets may lack a stable valuation basis. Their prices can be affected by supply and demand, market sentiment, regulatory news, technical events, issuer conduct, and macroeconomic conditions. Prices may rise or fall rapidly, and past performance does not represent future results.

2. Liquidity Risk

Some assets or trading pairs have limited buy and sell depth. During volatility or market stress, you may be unable to execute, close, or convert positions at your expected price or time. Larger orders may also materially affect the execution price.

3. Leverage and Contract Risk

Leverage magnifies both gains and losses. A small adverse market movement may lead to a margin call, forced liquidation, or loss of all margin.

Mark price, index price, funding rates, risk limits, insurance funds, and auto-deleveraging may affect position outcomes.

4. Order and Execution Risk

A market order does not guarantee execution at the most recently displayed price. Slippage, delay, partial execution, rejection, or order queuing may be affected by volatility, liquidity, connection quality, risk controls, and system capacity. Stop-loss and take-profit instructions also do not guarantee execution at the trigger price.

5. Technology and Network Risk

Internet interruptions, device failure, software errors, system maintenance, cyberattacks, API misconfiguration, or third-party failures may cause data delays, failed instructions, or temporary service unavailability.

Blockchains may also experience congestion, higher fees, forks, reorganizations, smart-contract vulnerabilities, or consensus failures. On-chain transfer confirmation time and finality are usually not controlled by GinX alone.

6. Custody and Account-Security Risk

Account takeover, phishing, SIM hijacking, malware, or credential leakage may cause asset loss. Independently verify domains and addresses, enable available multi-factor verification, and never disclose verification codes or private keys to anyone.

7. Stablecoin, Token, and Issuer Risk

Stablecoins may deviate from their target price. Token issuers may stop operating, change rules, or fail to perform commitments. Failures of reserves, redemption, governance, smart contracts, or cross-chain bridges may all cause losses.

Listing an asset on GinX does not represent a guarantee of its value, safety, legality, or future performance.

8. Real-World Synthetic Asset and Non-Crypto Underlying Risk

If GinX offers synthetic trading products referencing gold, foreign exchange, commodities, indices, or other real-world underlyings, those products do not necessarily represent direct ownership of the corresponding physical asset or underlying security.

Product prices may be affected by reference-market trading hours, liquidity, exchange rates, data providers, index calculation, market closures, abnormal conditions, and regulatory changes. When a reference market is closed, platform quotes and prices after reopening may differ or gap.

9. Third-Party Risk

Delays, errors, insolvency, or terms changes by payment institutions, banks, KYC providers, liquidity sources, data providers, or other third parties may affect the services. Third-party liability and GinX’s liability boundaries are governed by the applicable agreements.

10. Legal, Regulatory, and Tax Risk

The legal classification and regulatory requirements for digital assets may change rapidly. Changes may result in product restrictions, additional verification, trading suspensions, asset-disposal requirements, or tax consequences. You are responsible for understanding the legal and tax obligations applicable to you.

11. Copy-Trading Risk

A trading expert’s past performance does not represent future results. Followed orders may differ from the expert’s account because of balance, parameters, leverage, minimum order size, delay, slippage, or risk limits. Copy-trading tools do not eliminate trading risk, and you must independently decide whether to start, adjust, or stop copying.

12. Coupon, Bonus, and Campaign Reward Risk

Coupons, fee rebates, fee reductions, bonuses, trial funds, and other campaign rewards do not change the market risk of related trades and do not guarantee profit or loss compensation.

Relevant benefits may be limited by validity period, applicable orders, trading volume, holding period, account type, product scope, compensation cap, funding attributes, and anti-abuse rules. If conditions are not met, the validity period expires, or related transactions are found not to comply with campaign rules, some or all benefits may be unavailable or unsettled.

Bonuses or trial funds may not be directly withdrawable. Their usable scope and conditions for conversion into withdrawable balance are governed by the coupon terms and campaign rules.

13. Information and Non-Advice Statement

Prices, rankings, market commentary, educational materials, and user content may be incomplete, delayed, or contain errors. GinX does not provide investment advice based on your personal circumstances and does not guarantee the performance of any strategy or trading expert.

14. Risk Tolerance

Do not trade with funds needed for daily living, borrowed funds, or funds you cannot afford to lose. If you do not understand a product, rule, or worst-case outcome, do not use that product.
