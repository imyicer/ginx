Competition Rules & Terms

To maintain a fair and competitive environment, a strict zero-tolerance policy for violations will be enforced throughout the competition:

One Participant, One Account:

Each participant may register only once. Registrations sharing the same identity information, phone number, device ID (IMEI/MAC), or IP address will be considered the same entity. The use of VPNs or proxy servers to conceal identity and create multiple accounts is strictly prohibited. Any participant found operating multiple accounts will have all related accounts immediately disqualified.

Prohibited Trading Strategies and Arbitrage:

Malicious hedging and arbitrage activities are strictly prohibited. Participants may not use multiple demo accounts to execute internal A/B hedging strategies. Exploiting quote delays on demo servers (latency arbitrage) or trading based on abnormal/non-market pricing is forbidden. The use of High-Frequency Trading (HFT) scripts, scalping Expert Advisors (EAs), or any software/manual methods designed for risk-free or latency arbitrage is strictly prohibited.

Anti-Collusion Policy:

Participants are prohibited from colluding with others to place opposite trades across different accounts (distributed A/B hedging through multiple individuals). GinX's Risk Control Department will utilize order similarity analysis to monitor highly correlated trading activities, including execution timing, trade volume, and trade direction. Any confirmed collusion will result in the disqualification of all involved accounts.

Final Interpretation Rights:

GinX's Risk Control Department will continuously monitor all competition data. If any fraudulent, collusive, or rule-violating behavior is identified, the organizer reserves the right to immediately revoke participation eligibility and cancel any potential rewards. The organizing platform reserves the final right of interpretation of all competition rules.
